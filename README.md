# Damn him to hell, fuck me for giving a shit, thank you for coming: How Far Generalizations Reach in English

Brett Reynolds

**Under review at English Language and Linguistics. Submitted 24 November 2026.**

[Preprint with supplementary materials: LingBuzz 010557](https://ling.auf.net/lingbuzz/010557) · [Replication archive](preprint/replication-anonymized.zip). Posted 24 November 2026.

The current working article is `english-imprecatives-reframed.tex` ([PDF](english-imprecatives-reframed.pdf)); its supplementary materials are `english-imprecatives-supplement.tex` ([PDF](english-imprecatives-supplement.pdf)). The earlier `english-imprecatives.tex` is retained as a previous version.

Analysis scripts and saved aggregate results are in `analysis/` and `data/`. Restricted raw corpus files, internal reviews and the exact submission package are kept locally and excluded from this repository. Corpus-dependent extraction requires the relevant licensed inputs.

Build the current article with `make MAIN=english-imprecatives-reframed` and the supplement with `make MAIN=english-imprecatives-supplement`. XeLaTeX and Biber are required, together with the linked house-style resources.
