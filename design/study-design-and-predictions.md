# Pre-Specified Study Design and Competing Predictions

**Target journal:** *English Language and Linguistics* (*ELL*, $\le 10{,}000$ words inclusive of references and footnotes)
**Working title:** *Between Defective Verb and Schematic Construction: The English Maledictive and Benedictive NP Paradigm*
**Date:** 2026-11-22

---

## 1. Research Question

English has a productive family of subjectless, uninflected maledictive and benedictive utterances taking a bare noun-phrase object:

- *Damn these mosquitoes!* / *Damn you!* / *Damn it!*
- *Fuck you!* / *Fuck that!* / *Fuck me!*
- *Sod them all!* / *Sod this for a lark!*
- *Bugger the rest!* / *Bugger him!*
- *Blast those mosquitoes!* / *Curse you!* / *Screw the rules!* / *Hang the expense!*
- *Bless you!* / *Bless his heart!* / *Bless her cotton socks!*
- Minced heads: *Drat it!* / *Drat these mosquitoes!* / *Darn the luck!* / *Dang it!* / *Dash it all!*

Standard grammars disagree on how to analyse these forms: *CGEL* (Huddleston & Pullum 2002: 1361 n. 70) hedges toward an exceptional complement-taking interjection; Quang (1971/1992) posits a distinct word class of "quasi-verbs"; Quirk et al. (1985: 852–853) list them under irregular formulae; and constructionist/taboo-grammar accounts (Goldberg 2006; Napoli & Hoeksema 2009; Portner 2026) point to constructional licensing across taboo domains.

**Central empirical question:** Across contemporary British and North American English, how is grammatical labour divided between the **lexical head** and the **phrasal construction** in this paradigm?

## 3. Four Competing Hypotheses and Discriminating Predictions

### H1: Local Optative / Imperative Ellipsis
- **H1a (Ordinary 2sg imperative):** Already ruled out on the hostile addressee-object reading by Quang's (1971/1992) reflexive binding test (*Fuck you!* vs. *Assert yourself!* / *Go fuck yourself!*).
- **H1b (Head-specific recoverable-subject optative):** Rather than dismissing optative ellipsis uniformly across the whole family (as *CGEL* p. 1361 n. 70 does), test it **head by head** via the ratio of overt `God + HEAD + NP` to subjectless `HEAD + NP`.
  - **Prediction:** `bless` and `damn` will show robust overt `God`-subject alternation (`God bless NP`, `God damn NP`), whereas `fuck`, `sod`, `bugger`, `screw`, and minced `darn/drat/dang/dash` will show near-zero `God + HEAD + NP` (except `goddammit`/compound `God damn`), establishing a head-by-head asymmetry inside the paradigm.

### H2: Lexical Verbal Inheritance (Restricted / Defective Verbs)
- Heads are synchronically verbs inside a root construction blocking overt subject, tense, negation, passive, and ordinary adverbials; complement and PP properties are inherited from each head's lexical verbal entry.
- **Predictions:**
  1. **Head-specific NP selection:** Object types (pronoun vs. full lexical NP vs. body-part/possessive NP like *his heart*) and semantic readings will diverge sharply by head rather than pooling uniformly.
  2. **Head-specific PP selection:** Productive goal/reason PPs (`to hell`, `for V-ing`) will track each transparent verb's ordinary valency (distinguishing verb-selected PPs from idiomatic `for`-frames like *sod this for a lark* / *fuck this for an idea*).
  3. **Minced-head bottleneck:** Minced heads (`drat, darn, dash, dang`) lack live finite curse verbs (verified empirically by near-zero finite transitive curse tokens like *She dratted/darned/danged the X*), forcing H2 to treat them as a plain-form defective verbal subclass (unlike *beware*, which occurs in non-root infinitivals/modals and lacks participial `-ed` forms).

### H3: Lexical-Variant / Euphemistic Inheritance
- Minced forms (`darn, dang, dash, drat`) do not need a homophonous finite verb; instead, they inherit the **multi-constructional profile of the taboo lexeme they substitute for** (*damn*, or historical *God rot* analogized to *damn*).
- **Predictions (sharply distinct from H4!):**
  1. **Outside-construction mirroring:** Minced substitutes for *damn* (`darn`, `dang`, `darned`, `dratted`) will mirror *damn*'s **non-imprecative** constructions — attributive prenominal (`that darn/dang/dratted N`) and adverbial degree (`darn/dang/darned ADJ`) — at high rates, which nothing in a root `[X NP (PP)]` construction predicts.
  2. **Inside-construction mirroring:** Their bare-NP profile will mirror *damn*'s specifically (dominated by `it`-formulae and `you` + low variable full-NP share) rather than resembling *fuck/sod/bugger* or *bless*.

### H4: Schematic Constructional Licensing (`[X NP (PP)]`)
- Following Goldberg (2006) and Napoli & Hoeksema (2009), a schematic root maledictive/benedictive construction licenses bare-NP complementation across a conventional head class, independent of ordinary verbal valency.
- **Predictions:**
  1. **Uniform variable-NP licensing across transparent and minced heads:** Minced heads (`darn, dang, drat, dash`) will freely take **variable full lexical NPs** (not just fused `it`-formulae or caseless `you`), e.g. *Darn the luck!*, *Drat that cat!*, *Dang these mosquitoes!*, at non-trivial rates relative to their exclamatory uses.
  2. **Shared non-verbal or cross-head extensions:** Head-specific verbal valency will wash out in favour of construction-wide object constraints (target-class accessibility: *Damn/Fuck/Screw anyone who...* / *whoever...*) and shared `to hell` / `for`-PP patterns across heads whose ordinary verbs lack them.
  3. **Exclusion of primary interjections:** Unambiguous primary interjections (`ouch, oops, ugh, wow, whoops, yuck`) will show zero integrated bare-NP objects even when adjacent to NPs (where CABNC Jeffersonian prosodic/pause boundaries or vocative/exclamative parses separate them).

## 5. Coding Protocol (Eliminating R4–R5 Methodological Objections)

1. **Full recall check:** In Tier 1 local corpora, extract **every occurrence** of low/medium-frequency heads (and all candidate post-head NPs including proper names, bare plurals, and quantifiers via POS/context windows, not just `head + PRON/DET`).
2. **Explicit unit & subtype separation (never pooled blindly):**
   - `IT_FORMULA`: fused/resignative `HEAD it` (`damn it`, `dammit`, `fuck it`, `sod it`, `bugger it`, `darn it`, `drat it`, `dash it [all]`, `dang it`).
   - `ROUTINE_BLESS_YOU`: post-sneeze / thanking `Bless you`.
   - `PRON_2SG`: `HEAD you` (flagged separately because `you` is caseless and vocative-adjacent).
   - `PRON_NON2`: accusative `him, her, them, me, us` and demonstratives `this, that, these, those`.
   - `FULL_NP_LEX`: full lexical NP (`the/my/your/his/her/their/these/those + N`, proper name, bare plural, quantifier `everyone/all`, free relative `whoever...`), with sub-tag `POSS_BODY` for *his heart / her cotton socks / your eyes*.
   - `GOD_OPTATIVE`: overt-subject `God + HEAD + NP` (and `Lord/Heaven + HEAD + NP`).
   - `OUTSIDE_ATTR_ADV`: attributive (`that damn/darn/dang/dratted N`) and adverbial (`damn/darn/dang/darned ADJ`) uses.
   - `FINITE_VERB`: inflected/finite transitive verb (`damned/blasted/cursed/dratted/darned/danged` with overt subject + object, separating unrelated senses like `darn` 'mend' and `dash` 'rush').
   - `EXCLUDED`: ordinary literal verbs, nouns (`the poor sod`, `a damn`), standalone exclamation before finite clause (`Fuck, that would be amusing`), `what the fuck`, etc.
3. **Head-independent prosodic test in CABNC:** Inspect raw `.cha` tier strings between head and following NP for explicit pause/boundary cues (`(.)`, timed pause `(0.x)`, final contour `.` / `?` / `!`, capitalization of next TCU) across maledictive/benedictive tokens vs. primary interjections (`ouch, oops, ugh, oh, ah, hey`).
4. **Denominator hygiene:** Compute bare-NP rates both over **total lexeme tokens** AND over **exclamatory/imprecative-domain uses** (never extrapolating a pooled `it`-formula rate onto primary interjections!).
