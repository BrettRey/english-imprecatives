\section*{Corpus query strings and adjudication protocol}

Table~\ref{tab:appendix-queries} lists all Mark Davies queries (TV Corpus 325M; GloWbE 1.9B; LIST view). Tier~1 scanned all 13 heads and 6 primary interjections before closed-class NP starters (CABNC, OANC) and any NP-starting CLAWS5 tag (\texttt{PNP}, \texttt{AT0}, \texttt{DPS}, \texttt{DT0}, \texttt{NP0}, \texttt{NN1}, \texttt{NN2}, \texttt{PNI}, \texttt{CRD}) in BNC Baby XML.

Spoken BNC2014 (Sketch Engine \texttt{preloaded/bnc2014\_spoken\_tt3}, 11.83M tokens) ran eight lowercase CQL queries mirroring the same frames: \texttt{B2-1a} (\texttt{H13} + NP starters, 1,311 hits including 60 unaudited raw \mention{hang}; all 22 archaic/minced and 100/237 \texttt{DET/GEN} lines audited), \texttt{B2-1b} (\texttt{dammit\qsep{}goddammit}, 12), \texttt{B2-2} (6 \texttt{fuck}-euphemisms, all 11 audited), \texttt{B2-3} (\texttt{god} + heads, 17 hits: 10 \mention{god damn it} frequency-table strings and all 7 non-\mention{it} hits contextually audited), \texttt{B2-4a/b} (\texttt{to hell} [1] / \texttt{for .*ing} [3]), \texttt{B2-5a} (\texttt{confound}, 1), and \texttt{B2-5b} (\texttt{yay\qsep{}boo}, all 17 audited).
